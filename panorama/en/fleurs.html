A last tribute

Funeral flower arrangements to say what words cannot

When words fail, flowers speak for us. Choosing funeral flower arrangements is a last gesture of tenderness, gratitude or friendship for the person who has left us, and a little gentleness for those who remain.

At Murith, we know this choice comes at a moment when no one feels like comparing catalogues. Our advisors listen and guide you, from a small bouquet to a large composition. Florists can also deliver directly to our funeral rooms.

If the funeral notice says “no flowers or wreaths”, we respect that wish. Otherwise, the family is free to choose whatever feels right.

How to choose?

  • The wreath: round, often associated with eternity. It is often placed beside the coffin or on the grave.
  • The spray: more imposing, often given by friends and acquaintances to express sincere friendship.
  • The cushion or the bouquet: more intimate, often chosen by close family.
  • The colours: white shades for serenity, brighter ones to celebrate a life. Their meaning varies across cultures and religions: our advisors will guide you.
