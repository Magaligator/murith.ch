Our 10 services that ensure funerals take place in a climate of respect and peace

01 / 10

Complete funeral organisation in Geneva

Organising a funeral means juggling several things: the administrative formalities, the ceremony, the coffin, the flowers and informing relatives. All of this at once can become overwhelming during a period of mourning that is already difficult and isolating.

Murith funeral services takes care of you! A funeral advisor contacts you quickly after your first call. They listen to you and learn your needs, as well as those of the deceased. It is up to you to decide what type of ceremony you want: burial or cremation.

Then, we oversee every last detail on your behalf. Transport of the body, booking the ceremony, choosing the coffin, the flowers, press announcements and memorial cards. You can focus on what matters: spending time with your loved ones and grieving.

We are trained to support families of all cultures and religions. We adapt, whether you want a religious or secular ceremony, or one closest to your convictions. No two ceremonies are identical, because no two lives are identical. Our goal and our mission: to ensure that the end of life is worthy of the loved one.

02 / 10

Care of the deceased for a dignified burial

Once taken into our care, we treat the deceased with all the compassion and dignity you are entitled to expect. This is also a need for the family, because in this way they can begin the formalities in the best possible conditions.

The first thing we do is prepare for the mortuary toilette. The deceased is then washed and dressed in special clothing, usually chosen by the family. It is a small gesture, but an incredibly meaningful one. It allows your loved one to be presented the way they would have wished to be remembered.

We also offer embalming. This is when the body is preserved. In short, it helps the body stay in good condition for a few days. It is useful when the family needs time, for example to give distant relatives the chance to say goodbye. The family may choose to use this service or not, as it is entirely optional.

Once the body is prepared, it is placed in the coffin chosen by the family. This coffin can then be moved to one of our funeral chapels, where the family can come day and night to pay their respects.

Everything is done with discretion, gentleness and humanity. These difficult moments are no stranger to our teams. They know how to treat you with tact, and they do it with love.

03 / 10

Funeral ceremony of your choice: secular or religious

A funeral is the time to say our last goodbye, share stories, grieve together and begin the healing process.

Each ceremony organised by Murith funeral services is adapted to the family's instructions and the preferences of the deceased. Since no two lives are alike, it also means that no two ceremonies can be identical.

Would you like a religious ceremony?
We work with the church, the mosque, the temple, the synagogue and our partners in the name of all the saints.

Do you prefer a more personal, less ceremonial celebration?
We work with you to create an authentic reflection of them: songs they loved, carefully chosen words full of familiarity, as well as images and memories.

You have an experienced master of ceremonies. They lead the ceremony with discretion and kindness. They coordinate every participant: from the pallbearers to the flowers, including the music and the speakers. They make sure everything takes place with respect and dignity.

We also make the booking at our chapel at La Cluse, in a church, a crematorium or a cemetery in Geneva. Everything is arranged in advance so that, on the day of the services, you have nothing to manage.

04 / 10

Viewing rooms designed for your funeral

Our viewing rooms are calm and respectful places, designed so that the family can gather in complete peace. They are in the heart of Geneva, at 89 Boulevard de la Cluse, right opposite the cantonal hospital.

What makes our rooms unique is their accessibility. With an entry code, you can come at any hour of the day or night, 7 days a week. You do not need an appointment. You can come to our premises directly or contact us on +41 22 809 56 00.

Our viewing rooms are fully customisable. According to your cultural or religious traditions, we adapt the space: lighting, decoration, layout… The aim is for you to feel at ease and for the place to match who your loved one was.

We also have the La Cluse Chapel, a space dedicated to ceremonies. It suits both religious and secular ceremonies. It is a place that welcomes families with kindness and without judgement.

Whether you want a vigil, a moment of silent reflection, or a more formal ceremony before the burial, our spaces are suited to every situation. Our teams make sure everything is ready when you arrive.

05 / 10

Made-to-measure coffins for the burial

The coffin is one of the central elements of a funeral. It is often one of the first things the family has to choose. At Murith funeral services, we offer a wide choice so that you find what best matches your wishes and your budget.

We work with many types of wood: oak, pine, cherry, mahogany and more. Each wood has its own colour, its own texture, its own character. You choose the one that suits you, according to your preferences and your traditions.

The finishes are also varied. Natural varnish, lacquered, matt or glossy… Every detail can be adapted. The handles, the interior lining, the ornaments: everything is designed to honour your loved one in a dignified and beautiful way.

Our funeral advisor will present the different options during your appointment. They are not trying to sell you the most expensive one. They are trying to help you find what is right for you. They answer all your questions patiently and without technical jargon.

For families who wish to opt for cremation, we also offer coffins suited to this type of funeral. They comply with current standards and respect the environment.

06 / 10

Flowers for the funeral

Flowers have always been part of funerals. They are a sign of respect, love and remembrance. Murith funeral services in Geneva offer floral decorations that enhance the ceremony's setting and show respect for the deceased.

Whether you are looking for a simple, discreet bouquet or a large floral arrangement to place on the coffin, we have options for every taste and every budget. You compose with care, according to tradition, or as you wish.

The flowers will adorn the coffin and the funeral room, or be given to relatives as a keepsake. Other families choose flowers with a special meaning for the deceased: their favourite flowers, the colours they loved. It is a simple and delicate way to personalise the ceremony.

On the day of the ceremony, we take care of the order, delivery and installation. You have nothing to prepare on your side. You choose, we handle the rest.

If you are not yet sure what you wish to select, our team is at your service. It knows the procedures in force in Geneva and can guide you according to your budget and expectations. The essential thing is that flowers are present to embellish this moment and pay tribute to the deceased.

07 / 10

Funeral repatriation: funerals abroad

When a death occurs in Switzerland and the family wishes the funeral to take place in the deceased's country of origin, it is a complex process. Official papers, authorities, embassies and transport all have to be handled. It is far too much to manage alone at such a difficult time.

Murith funeral services takes care of everything! Thanks to our long experience in international funeral transport, we are in direct contact with foreign representations in Switzerland: consulates, embassies, local authorities. We know the steps to follow for each country and handle them quickly.

We organise the transport of the deceased by air or by road, depending on the destination and the family's wishes. For air transport, we book the flight to the airport closest to the place of burial. If the family wishes to travel on the same flight as the deceased, we do everything possible to reserve seats for them.

For road transport, the deceased can be accompanied directly to the home town, without stopovers. A family member can, if desired, travel in the vehicle.

Before departure, it is also possible to organise a farewell ceremony in our La Cluse chapel, for relatives who are in Geneva.

08 / 10

Administrative formalities following a death

When a death occurs, all the formalities have to be completed. Very often it is impossible to manage everything while you are still in the emotional state you are in. Murith funeral services supports you at every step, leaving nothing to chance.

First of all, there is the declaration of death. It must be registered with the civil registry of the municipality or place where the person died, within two days.

You then need to obtain the death certificate. This is the official document attesting the death. It differs from the medical certificate. It states where and when the person died and is regularly required by banks, insurers and other institutions. We recommend requesting several copies; we also do this for you.

In the same way, we inform you about all the people and organisations that must be notified of the death: the employer, the landlord or owner, insurance companies, your AVS/AHV compensation fund, banks and tax authorities. And even social networks, which many families forget.

If a will was drawn up, we remind you that it must be handed over to the competent institution without delay. If you need it, we can put you in touch with the appropriate contacts.

09 / 10

Death notices and funeral announcements

One of the most emotionally heavy things you can do is announce a death to your loved ones. Do you know how all the words fit together, where you must publish a notice, and how to think of everyone who needs to be involved? All of this can seem immense.

At Murith Funeral Services Geneva, we help you write and publish the funeral notice as well as memorial cards. In the Geneva newspaper, the regional press or elsewhere: you simply tell us where to publish.

Thank-you cards are also sent to family members, friends and colleagues. If you wish, we draw up the list and send it to everyone.

We make sure the announcement is made in time so that family and friends can be informed and prepare to attend the funeral. It is a modest service, but it brings immense comfort to families at this difficult time.

10 / 10

Plan your own funeral in advance

We rarely think about it, but preparing your own funeral in advance is one of the most precious gifts you can give to your loved ones. It spares them from making difficult decisions in the pain of grief.

At Murith funeral services, you can organise every detail of your funeral in advance. The type of ceremony you want, secular or religious. The music you love. The flowers you prefer. The place of burial. Your last wishes are recorded and kept in a safe place.

Your loved ones will no longer have to wonder what you would have wanted. Everything is already decided, according to your wishes, with your agreement. It is a way of easing their grief and offering them a little serenity at the most difficult time.

Funeral pre-planning is also a way to control the cost of your funeral. By planning ahead, you can choose what suits your current budget, without leaving this burden to your loved ones.

To learn more about our pre-planning options, visit our dedicated site: www.murithprevoyance.ch or contact us directly. Our advisor will see you with no commitment, at your home or at our offices.
