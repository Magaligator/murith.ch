Frequently asked questions

What are the administrative steps following a death?

Administrative formalities in Geneva require the declaration of the death, obtaining a death certificate and contacting financial institutions to block and unblock the deceased's accounts. Our funeral service takes care of everything related to the deceased and the administrative steps.

See the steps to follow

What does organising a funeral in the canton of Geneva involve?

Organising a funeral in the Canton of Geneva involves preparing the body, planning the ceremony and choosing between burial and cremation.

Discover our funerals

What is a death certificate?

It is a document proving the death, giving details about the deceased and stating where and when the death occurred. Murith funeral services takes care of obtaining this document.

See the steps to follow

What are the steps in preparing the body?

Preparing the body is a crucial stage in organising a funeral. Murith funeral services, based in Geneva, offer specialised services for preparing the deceased, including mortuary care, dressing and placing in the coffin.

Thanatopraxy, a form of body preservation, is an optional practice available at the family's request. This care plays an essential role for families who wish to extend the period of reflection before burial or cremation.

Discover our funerals

What services do our funeral directors offer?

The Murith funeral company offers a range of services such as organising funerals, transporting and preserving the body, and all related funeral products – coffins, flower arrangements – as well as preparing death notices and announcements.

Discover our funerals

Why choose Murith funeral services?

Organising a funeral in Geneva can be a complex task, with many aspects to consider. However, with proper planning and the help of skilled professionals, the process can be less stressful and more manageable. Murith funeral services have more than 135 years of experience supporting bereaved families.
