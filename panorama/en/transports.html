Repatriations

Fast handling, deadlines respected, by air or by road

The relationships we maintain with foreign representations in Switzerland and our company's long experience of these transports allow us to be operational quickly to carry out the steps needed for a repatriation. As quickly as possible, we organise the return of a deceased person to their country of origin, wherever in Switzerland they died.

If the family so wishes, our chapel allows a ceremony to be held before departure. Depending on the destination, we book the flight to the airport closest to the place of burial. If necessary, we try to contact the airline to pre-book seats for the family on the same flight. Road transport makes it possible to go directly, without transfer, to the deceased's home town. Where applicable, a family member can accompany the deceased in the vehicle.

How do we work?

01

All death formalities taken care of

We contact the authorities (police, consulate, embassy and others).

02

Body preservation care

This consists of providing hygiene care so that the deceased is preserved in the best possible conditions until arrival at the destination.

03

Transport booking

We organise the transfer of the body, whether by air or by car, within very short deadlines.
