Understanding the cost of a funeral in Geneva: advice and estimate of expenses

Cost of a funeral29 Apr 2024

What is the cost of a funeral? This worrying question is often raised in the most difficult moments. As a general rule, in Switzerland, expect costs ranging from CHF 3,000 to 10,000. In this article we offer an overview of the essential expenses, the optional choices that can affect your budget, and recommendations for striking a balance between respecting last wishes and keeping a controlled budget.

1. Administrative steps and their costs

In Geneva, after a death, there are several administrative steps to take. These include obtaining a medical death certificate, declaring the death at the town hall, and obtaining the death certificate. These steps have been governed by the cantonal regulation since 1 June 2009, which provides a legal framework for post-death administrative procedures.

When you go through Murith funeral services, all death formalities are taken care of to ease your burden at this difficult time.

2. Choosing between burial and cremation

Another key factor that can influence the cost of a funeral is the choice between burial and cremation. This choice can have significant implications for the funeral budget. However, it is important to understand the costs associated with each option in order to make an informed choice and make the decision easier.

The price of a burial

If you opt for a burial, there are several costs to consider. The first is the coffin, then the purchase of a concession and a plot in a Geneva cemetery for 20 years, with the possibility of renewing this concession.

It is important to note that these costs can vary depending on the length of the concession and the location of the cemetery.

The cost of a cremation

If you opt for a cremation, the cost can also vary depending on several factors. The choice of coffin and urn are the main factors influencing the cost of a cremation.

It is also important to note that crematoria in Switzerland do not accept cardboard coffins.

3. Analysis of funeral costs in Geneva

In Geneva, funeral costs can vary depending on several elements, and can differ from one funeral service to another. These elements include:

  • funeral supplies (coffins)
  • third-party services (crematorium, cemetery, death notices, etc.)
  • taxes linked to the funeral
  • flower arrangements
  • a gathering after the funeral service (funeral ceremony, reception, etc.)

To obtain an accurate cost estimate, it is advisable to contact a funeral services company located in the canton of Geneva. These companies have in-depth knowledge of the costs and can help you understand the expenses linked to the funeral.

Planning a funeral involves many decisions and varied costs. It is therefore essential to understand what the unavoidable expenses are when organising a funeral.

Unavoidable expenses

The first unavoidable expense when planning a funeral is the coffin. In Switzerland, the cost of a fir coffin is around CHF 1,000. For example, at Murith funeral services a fir coffin costs CHF 940, but the cost of a coffin can vary depending on the type of wood and quality. For some examples of coffins, click here.

When planning a funeral, the cost of a suitable coffin ranges from CHF 1,000 to CHF 10,000 for more elaborate models. It is therefore important to take these costs into account when planning the funeral.

Optional funeral services

In addition to the unavoidable expenses, there are also optional funeral services that can increase the total cost of a funeral. These services may include a thanatopractor, the hire of a funeral home for the last farewell, and the publication of death notices. It is important to note that these services can vary from one company to another.

Murith funeral services have more than 135 years of experience in the funeral sector and enjoy an excellent reputation and expertise passed down from father to son for 5 generations.

Optional services during a funeral can also involve:

  • a celebrant to personalise the farewell and pay tribute to the deceased
  • musicians to play live music
  • additional flowers to decorate the ceremony venue
  • photography or videography services to capture the important moments of the ceremony
  • These additional services can add a personal touch to the ceremony, but can also increase the total cost.

4. Flower arrangements for funerals in Geneva

Flower arrangements are another expense to take into account when planning a funeral. The price of flower arrangements for a funeral in Geneva generally ranges between CHF 100 and CHF 900.

Flower arrangements are an integral part of the funeral ceremony and can be personalised according to your wishes. Whether you opt for a simple wreath or a more complex floral arrangement, it is important to take these costs into account when setting your funeral budget. To see the prices of flower arrangements in Geneva, click here.

5. Funeral rooms

A funeral room is a place where the body of the deceased is kept before the funeral and burial. For example, at Murith funeral services we have set up funeral rooms available to families and their relatives 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

If the death occurs in a care home, a clinic or a hospital, the deceased is transferred within hours by the funeral service in Geneva to their premises.

6. In summary, the cost of funerals

To conclude, understanding the costs associated with a funeral can help you plan and manage expenses effectively. Whether it is choosing between burial and cremation, understanding the unavoidable expenses or the optional funeral services, it is essential to be informed in order to make sound choices.
