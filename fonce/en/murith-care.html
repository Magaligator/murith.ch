Murith Care Everywhere

Why Murith Care Everywhere?

With Murith Care Everywhere, Murith Funeral Services supports Swiss families living abroad by offering a complete funeral pre-planning solution. This service ensures the repatriation of the deceased to Geneva through a network of partner companies, before we take charge of the funeral in Switzerland or France.

We guarantee strict respect for the wishes of the deceased, in accordance with the quote drawn up, while offering a more accessible and economical alternative.

Murith Care Everywhere is the promise of human and professional support, so that every family can face this delicate moment with serenity.

How does it work?

  1. 1Step 1

    Meeting the family on site abroad.

  2. 2Step 2

    Identification of providers according to the family's needs.

  3. 3Step 3

    The negotiated quote is handed in person to the family at Murith's premises. + 41 22 809 56 00

  4. 4Step 4

    Payment for the services is made on site, directly at the time of death.

  5. 5Step 5

    Administrative formalities begin and the deceased is taken into care.

  6. 6Step 6

    Once in Geneva, Murith carries out the contract in accordance with the wishes set out.
