Our models

Here are our thank-you card models

Texts

We offer a few sample texts:

Text A

The family of
Mr/Mrs…………..
deeply touched by all the expressions of sympathy and affection shown to them in their great bereavement, thanks you most sincerely and asks you to accept here the expression of their heartfelt gratitude

Text B

Deeply touched by the expressions of sympathy and affection received on the death of
Mr/Mrs…………..
his/her family thanks you most sincerely for your presence, your gifts, your flowers, your messages of condolence and asks you to accept here the expression of their heartfelt gratitude.

Text C

With all their heart and deep emotion, the family of
Mr/Mrs…………..
thanks you for the affection and support you showed them in their immense grief. They ask you to believe in their heartfelt gratitude.
