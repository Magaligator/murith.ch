Our viewing rooms

Tailor-made arrangements

Our rooms can be personalised: you can add photos, videos and music, and florists can make deliveries there. The family has access 24/7 by means of a code. Murith funeral services has its own La Cluse chapel, which can welcome up to 60 people with the possibility of a video projection. We work with respect for all religions: our advisors can support you in personalising a religious or secular ceremony or a musical moment of reflection.
