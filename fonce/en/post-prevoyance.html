Funeral Pre-Planning Geneva: Definition and Foundations

What is funeral pre-planning?

Funeral pre-planning is a contract by which a person organises and pre-finances their own funeral during their lifetime. Unlike funeral insurance, which pays a sum to beneficiaries with no restriction on its use, funeral pre-planning guarantees precise execution of the policyholder's wishes: type of ceremony (secular or religious), choice of coffin, method of burial (interment or cremation), venue of the ceremony, flowers, music, etc.

At Maison Murith, the funds paid under a funeral pre-planning contract in Geneva are secured in an independent structure. In the event of unforeseen circumstances, the agreed services are guaranteed or the funds returned according to the terms of the contract.

The Cost of Funerals in Geneva: What Murith Pre-Planning Covers

In French-speaking Switzerland, the cost of a funeral is often underestimated. Simple funerals start at around CHF 3,000, while a traditional and personalised ceremony can reach CHF 10,000 to 15,000. The average is between CHF 4,500 and 10,500 depending on the services chosen.

A Murith funeral pre-planning contract locks in prices at the time of subscription, thus protecting you from future inflation. You can opt for payment in full or by instalments according to your means.

The Public Funeral Service of the City of Geneva

The City of Geneva has a public funeral service unique in French-speaking Switzerland, created in 1866. This service offers free funerals to Geneva residents for basic services (poplar coffin, dressing, placing in the coffin, transfer, burial or cremation). However, since 2014 this service no longer accepts new funeral pre-plans.

Important: outside the City of Geneva, only the municipality of Vernier offers partial coverage under certain conditions. In the other Geneva municipalities, families must bear the full cost. This is where funeral pre-planning makes all its sense.

Trends and Developments in Swiss Funeral Pre-Planning

In Switzerland, around 10% of deaths are currently covered by a funeral pre-planning contract, a rate that is still low compared with France (25%) or the Netherlands (85%). The sector is growing strongly, driven by a collective awareness of the financial and organisational issues linked to death.

The national trend is clearly towards cremation, which accounts for more than 80% of burials in many Swiss cantons. This development directly affects pre-planning contracts, which must adapt to increasingly varied wishes: scattering of ashes in nature, biodegradable urns, personalised ceremonies.

Maison Murith supports these developments while keeping what is essential: deeply human support, because death remains a personal and intimate subject that deserves sincere and individual attention.
